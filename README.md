# Restoration Protocol

A 10-minute roguelite about stubborn robots planting life on a frozen planet — knowing the ice storm will destroy everything, and starting again anyway.

## Concept

The world is falling apart, and you want to do something about it — but you don't know what. You want control back. You want to feel like your understanding of the world isn't complete garbage.

**Setting:** A frozen planet. Abandoned robots reactivate under a restoration program. Their mission: grow plants, restore nature. They don't understand why. They just execute the protocol — over and over, session after session, storm after storm.

**Metaphor:** This isn't a story about hope. It's a story about stubbornness against the indifferent universe. Hope that doesn't die — not because it's brave, but because it's programmed. The robots are us: grinding through cycles, building from the ashes of past failures, unable to stop trying even when the odds are cosmically unfair.

## Art Style

Neon outlines on pure black. No fills. The line itself carries all emotion and information.

**Line as expression:**
- **Trembling, broken lines** — fragility, the world barely holding together. Ice cracks rendered as interrupted strokes with gaps that scream emptiness
- **Rough, jagged lines with scratches and dents** — the worn-out robots. A bolt drawn as an incomplete circle. A body contour that breaks and resumes with an offset
- **Bold, confident, pulsing lines** — life. Plant stems that reach upward. Leaves that unfurl like open palms. Flower contours with tiny serrations — life beating from within. These lines are thicker and brighter than everything else on screen
- **Chaotic, overlapping slashes** — the storm. Ghost-lines (pale shifted echoes) appear as the storm approaches. Plant contours gain counter-directional hatching as wind tears at them. At peak storm: no contours at all, just short directionless dashes flying across the screen

**Storm phases change line behavior:**
1. **Calm** — clean, distinct outlines. Warm neon colors pulse gently
2. **Storm approaching** — lines start doubling, trembling. Colors desaturate at screen edges
3. **Storm** — all structure dissolves into abstract monochrome chaos
4. **After storm** — pristine silence. One thin, clear arc: a new sprout. Robots half-buried, stirring back to life

## Core Loop

1. **Emerge** — Robot crawls out of a cave
2. **Place base** — Set down a heat generator. It thaws 4 adjacent slots
3. **Plant and build** — Use thawed slots for plants or structures
4. **Expand** — Buy more heat generators to thaw more territory. Each new generator demands exponentially more energy. The further from center, the hungrier the appetite
5. **Race** — ~10 minutes of frantic optimization. Plants grow, produce biomass, robots harvest. Every decision is a trade-off: go deep on one plot or spread thin across many?
6. **Ice storm** — Everything dies. The storm doesn't care about your plans
7. **Restart** — Robots reactivate. Restoration protocol engaged. Again. But this time, with permanent upgrades earned from previous cycles

The tension: expanding territory costs exponential energy. A fifth generator might need two plants just to sustain itself — but those plants need time to grow, and the storm is already on the horizon. Do you overextend and risk collapse, or play it safe and harvest less?

## Economy

One resource rules them all: **Biomass** — organic matter harvested from plants or scavenged from what the storm killed.

Three conversion paths, one agonizing question: *"What do you build from the ashes of your failures?"*

### Biofuel (Burn the past to survive now)
Convert biomass into energy to feed heat generators. Keeps the lights on, buys more time this session. Burns away forever. A desperate, present-tense act of survival — trading the memory of past failures for five more minutes of warmth.

### Structural Materials (Build for next time)
Convert biomass into permanent upgrades for robots and buildings. Faster hands, tougher frames, more efficient generators. An investment in tools — building scaffolding from the bones of fallen hopes. Progress purchased with yesterday's corpses.

### Lab Synthesis (Change the game forever)
The most expensive path. Feed biomass into laboratories that research genetic upgrades for plants: faster growth, frost resistance, richer yields, new species. Low probability, high cost, but the only path to permanent evolution of the seed bank. Alchemy — extracting the soul from dead matter.

Every session, the player faces the same brutal allocation problem: burn it all to protect one precious flower right now? Invest in infrastructure for a stronger next run? Or pour everything into the lab, gambling on a breakthrough that could change all future cycles?
