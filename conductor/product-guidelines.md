# Product Guidelines: Restoration Protocol

## Communication Principle

**Show, don't tell.** No text in the game. Everything is communicated through visuals, animations, numbers, and icons. The player learns by watching and doing, not by reading.

## In-Game Text Policy

- **No tutorial text.** Mechanics are taught through visual feedback and progressive disclosure
- **No flavor text.** No descriptions on buildings, plants, or upgrades
- **No dialog.** Robots don't talk. The world speaks through its behavior
- **Numbers only where essential.** Biofuel amount, costs, production rates — raw numbers, no labels where an icon suffices
- **Icons over words.** Every resource, building, and action is represented by an icon, never a text label

## Visual Language

### Line as Communication

The art style itself IS the communication layer:

- **Plant health:** Line thickness and pulse intensity show vitality. Dying plants — lines thin, flicker, break apart
- **Economy state:** Biofuel bar color shifts from warm (safe) to cold (danger). No text warning needed
- **Storm approach:** Background lines multiply, tremble, desaturate. Player feels it before the numbers confirm it
- **Robot state:** Working robots have confident, smooth motion. Idle robots — subtle twitch, waiting posture

### Feedback Through Animation

- **Successful action:** Brief glow, satisfying snap into place
- **Failed action / can't afford:** Shake, flash red, bounce back
- **Production:** Visible fruit flow from plant → robot → drill. The chain is self-explanatory
- **Upgrade:** Visual transformation of the building — bigger, brighter lines, more detail
- **Collapse:** Neon fades line by line. Silence. Then drill sound from below

### Color as Status

| Color | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Warm neon (green, cyan) | Healthy, producing, safe |
| Cool neon (blue, purple) | Cold, frozen, dormant |
| Red / orange pulse | Danger, drain exceeding income |
| White flash | Action feedback, damage |
| Dimming / desaturation | Storm approach, energy loss |

## UI Design Principles

- **Minimal chrome.** UI should feel like part of the world, not overlaid on it
- **Bottom panel:** Cards with icons and numbers. No text labels — icons are self-descriptive
- **Resource bar:** A single bar or counter. Color communicates state
- **No tooltips on first play.** If something needs a tooltip to be understood, redesign the icon
- **Progressive complexity:** Early game shows minimal UI. New elements appear as player unlocks them

## Audio as Communication

- **Ambient shifts** signal storm phases (calm → rising → peak)
- **Mechanical sounds** for robot actions — no voice, no text pop-ups
- **Musical tension** replaces any "warning" text
- **Silence** after collapse is the most powerful "message"

## Brand Identity

- **Name:** Restoration Protocol
- **Visual signature:** Neon outlines on black. The void is as important as the line
- **Emotional tone:** Quiet stubbornness. Not dramatic, not heroic — mechanical persistence
- **No logo text needed.** The drill silhouette + sprout outline IS the brand mark
